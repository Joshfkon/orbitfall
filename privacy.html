PRIVACY

OrbitfallSupport

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version

Orbitfall does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, campaign progress and saved replays are stored only on your device. They're removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

Online play uses Apple's Game Center. Matchmaking, invitations and your Game Center name are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. During a match, the game sends moves between players' devices through Game Center; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

The Commander's Pack is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Children

Because nothing is collected, Orbitfall is suitable for players of all ages under this policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: joshuafkon@yahoo.com.
