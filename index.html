Orbitfall is fast, elegant real-time strategy for iPhone. Tap a world, tap a target, and send your swarm across the stars. Face clever AI rivals, play friends online with Game Center, forge and break alliances in the Fog of War, and with the Commander's Pack take on the Hive and the hidden regions of deep space.

Free to play. No ads, no timers.

Joining the beta

  1. On your iPhone, tap Join the beta above.
  2. If asked, install Apple's free TestFlight app, then come back and tap the button again.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. Orbitfall appears on your home screen.

The Commander's Pack is free during the beta. Send feedback from inside TestFlight: take a screenshot in the game and tap Share Beta Feedback.
