How do I play?

Tap one of your worlds to select it, then tap any other world to send half its ships there. The slider at the bottom changes how much each order sends. Double-tap to select all your worlds, drag to aim, and touch and hold any world to learn about it. The menu's How to Play replays the guided tutorial.

Restoring the Commander's Pack

On a new device, tap any locked mode on the menu to open the store, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Playing online

Online play uses Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then tap Online and invite friends. Bots fill any empty seats, and if a player leaves, a bot takes over their worlds.

My numbers are hidden

That's by design: you can't see how many ships a rival world holds. Beacon worlds and radar reveal them nearby, and allies share what they see.
